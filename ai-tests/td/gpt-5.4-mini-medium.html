Merge Siege

Build a 3D defense grid, fuse matching towers into stronger forms, and decide how far to press the wave chain before banking out. Every run is different, every choice has a cost, and the best reward often arrives right after the point you should have quit.

Core loop: place towers on build pads, fuse adjacent duplicates, survive a wave, take a rogue-like upgrade, then choose whether to cash out or push for the next multiplier.

How it works

  • Tap or click a build pad to place the selected tower. Same type and tier will fuse when adjacent.
  • Use sell mode to clear bad placements and rebuild the board into a stronger merge chain.
  • After each wave, choose one breakthrough card or skip it and push on. Higher chain multipliers mean bigger rewards and harder enemies.
  • At any intermission, cash out to bank your run. The tension comes from deciding when the marginal reward no longer beats the risk.
