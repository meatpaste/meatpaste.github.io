How to Play

  • Tap a tower in the tray, then tap an empty green tile to place it.
  • Tap a tower to select it, then tap another tower of the same type & level to merge them into a stronger tower.
  • Tap a selected tower, then an empty tile, to move it.
  • Select a tower and hit Sell for a partial gold refund.
  • Enemies walk the sandy path — don't let them reach the end or you lose lives.
  • Clear a wave to draft a relic, gold bonus, or risky gamble.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom. Runs end when lives hit zero — earn Gems to buy permanent upgrades.
