HOW TO BEND THE RIFT

FORGE & POSITION

Forge random towers into empty pads. Tap a tower, then an empty pad to move it. Combat moves briefly recalibrate the tower.

MERGE & ASCEND

Merge equal tiers; the receiver keeps its class. A full board can cascade-forge, and paired Tier V towers Ascend without a power cap.

COUNTER THE RIFT

Blasters erase runners, Mortars clear swarms, and Tesla strips shields. The Rift predicts over-specialized builds before each wave.

WAGER

Rupture and Cataclysm waves are harder but pay far more. Every two clears earn an Echo; every fifth opens a vault: secure, stake, or extract.

OVERCLOCK

Select a tower and overclock once per wave for a huge burst, followed by a brief shutdown. Timing matters.
