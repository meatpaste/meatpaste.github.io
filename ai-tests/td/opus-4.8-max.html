HOW TO PLAY

  • 🔷 Summon places a random Tier-1 tower on a free plot. Cost rises with how many towers you own — so keep the board tidy.
  • 🔀 Merge: drag one tower onto another of the same type & tier to fuse them into the next tier. Power roughly doubles each tier — merging is the only way to keep up.
  • ↔️ Drag onto an empty plot to move; onto a different tower to swap.
  • 🌊 Start Wave early to claim a time bonus (🪙 per second left) — greed vs. safety.
  • 💰 Unspent gold earns interest between waves. Bank it or spend it.
  • 🎴 After each wave, draft a relic or skip it for gold.
  • 💎 When your Keep falls you earn Shards → spend on Ascension upgrades for a stronger next run. Chase your best wave.
  • 🖐️ Drag empty space to orbit, pinch / scroll to zoom. Tap a tower for stats.
