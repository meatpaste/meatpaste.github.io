HOW TO PLAY

Build: Tap a tower in the shop, then tap an empty tile to place it. Towers auto-fire at foes on the path.

Merge: Drag one tower onto another of the same type & level to fuse into a higher level — the core of your power. Drag to an empty tile to move; drag onto a different tower to swap.

Economy: You earn interest on saved gold each wave — hold vs. spend is the game. Clearing waves pays bonuses.

Boons: Each wave, draft 1 of 3 upgrades. Some carry risk. Reach deep to escape with more ⬦ Shards, spent on permanent Ascension upgrades. Come back for the Daily Challenge — same seed for everyone.
