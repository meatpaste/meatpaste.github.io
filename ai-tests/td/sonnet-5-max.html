How to Play

Tap a glowing open tile to build the tower currently in your hand.

Drag a tower onto another of the same type & level to merge it into something stronger.

Defend the Keep as waves march down the path. Between waves, choose a Blessing and spend gold at the shop.

When the Keep falls, the Essence you earned stays forever — spend it in the Sanctum to grow stronger for your next run.
